Traceable Digital Pipeline

Standard University Approval Workflow

From initial student application to final departmental decision and balance synchronization.

5-Stage Structured Workflow
1
Submission

Student signs in, selects leave type, dates, reason, emergency contact, and attaches documents.

2
Validation

System validates working days, holiday exclusions, quota balances, and detects overlapping leaves.

3
Faculty Review

Class In-charge inspects details, marks approval, rejects with remarks, or requests more information.

4
HOD Escalation

Medical leaves & extended requests escalate to Head of Department for final institutional approval.

5
Decision & Sync

Student receives in-app/email alerts; balance is updated and record is logged in immutable audit trail.
